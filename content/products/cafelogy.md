## What It Is

Cafelogy is a campaign management tool built for cafes. It helps cafe owners plan and run marketing campaigns and build a consistent brand presence, both online and in their local community.

## Who It's For

Cafe owners and small hospitality brands who want to market consistently but don't have the time or team to run campaigns themselves.

## Status

Currently helping cafes build their presence and iterating on the product.
