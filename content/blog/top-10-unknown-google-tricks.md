## Google Tricks

## The TOP TEN GOOGLE TRICKS :-

## **Definitions**

Pull up the definition of the word by typing define followed by the word you want the definition for. For example, typing: **define bravura** would display the definition of that word.

![Definitions - Google Tricks ](http://i1.wp.com/www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/download-2.jpg?resize=321%2C157)

## Local search

Visit [Google Local](http://local.google.com/) enter the area you want to search and the keyword of the place you want to find. For example, typing: **restaurant** at the above link would display local restaurants.

## Google Tricks

## Phone number lookup

Enter a full phone number with area code to display the name and address associated with that phone number.  
![google tricks ](http://i2.wp.com/www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/download-3.jpg?resize=271%2C186)

## Find weather and movies

Type “weather” or “movies” followed by a zip code or city and state to display current weather conditions or movie theaters in your area. For example, typing **weather 84101** gives you the current weather conditions for Salt Lake City, UT and the next four days. Typing **movies 84101** would give you a link for show times for movies in that area.

![Google tricks ](http://i0.wp.com/www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/download-4.jpg?resize=244%2C207)

## Track airline flight and packages

Enter the airline and flight number to display the status of an airline flight and it’s arrival time. For example, type: **delta 123** to display this flight information if available.

Google can also give a direct link to package tracking information if you enter a UPS, FedEx or USPS tracking number.  
![Google tricks ](http://i1.wp.com/www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/images-15.jpg?resize=201%2C161)

## Translate

Translate text, a full web page, or search by using the **[Google Language Tools](http://www.google.com/language_tools)**.

## Pages linked to you

See what other web pages are linking to your website or blog by typing link: followed by your URL. For example, typing **link:http://www.itvizkids.com** or Simply **link:itvizkids** displays all pages linking to IT-Vizkids .  
![Google tricks ](http://i2.wp.com/www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/images-16.jpg?resize=283%2C178)

## Find PDF results only

Add fileType: to your search to display results that only match a certain file type. For example, if you wanted to display PDF results only type: **“dell xps” fileType:pdf** – this is a great way to find online manuals.  
![Google tricks ](http://i2.wp.com/www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/download-6.jpg?resize=359%2C140)

## Calculator

Use the Google Search engine as a calculator by typing a math problem in the search. For example, typing: **100 + 200** would display results as 300 Or Simple type Calculator in the Google Search Bar and get a Instant Calculator on the Search Result Page:-  
![Google tricks ](http://i0.wp.com/www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/download-7.jpg?resize=272%2C185)

## Stocks

Quickly get to a stock quote price, chart, and related links by typing the stock symbol in Google. For example, typing **stock:msft** will display the stock information for Microsoft.  
![Google tricks ](http://i2.wp.com/www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/download-8.jpg?resize=278%2C182)
