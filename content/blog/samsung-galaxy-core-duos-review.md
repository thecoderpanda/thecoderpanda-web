Samsung’s New Gadget , marks the Release of Yet Another Galaxy Device.

Samsung’s new mid-range Dual SIM Android smartphone, Samsung Galaxy Core Duos is now available in Indian market. Samsung Galaxy Core Duos Price is Rs 15,199.

Samsung Galaxy Core Duos has a 4.3-inch TFT LCD display with a screen resolution of 480 x 800  and it is powered by a 1.2GHz dual-core processor with 1GB RAM. The smartphone runs on Google Android 4.1.2 Jelly Bean OS.

So Now Let’s have a Look at their Specifications and Know this Device  Briefly .

This New Galaxy Device has 5 megapixel rear camera, VGA front camera, 8GB of internal storage and upto 32GB of expandable memory via microSD Card.

Galaxy Core Duos has Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, 3G, microUSB, microUSB v2.0 and it is powered by a 1800mAh battery which gives up to 500 hours of standby time and upto 14 hours of Talk time.
