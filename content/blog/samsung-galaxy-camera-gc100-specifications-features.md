**Introduction:**  
With numerous attempts by [camera](http://www.naaptol.com/buy/cameras.html) manufacturers to offer something innovative and completely different from the rests, Samsung takes the lead. Its cameras are often highly innovative, but the Samsung Galaxy Camera GC100 is an amazing creation. It combines a camera and an Android media player into one device. Let us gain some more knowledge about this new gadget in Samsung Galaxy Series.

## Samsung Galaxy Camera GC100

![Samsung Galaxy Camera](http://www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/download-10.jpg)

**Design and Built:**  
Galaxy Camera GC100 accommodating more hardware than a typical point and shoot camera comes with dimensions of 128.7×70.8×19.1mm and weighs approximately 300 grams. This Galaxy Camera comes in a white metallic body with the rubber coated right side. The height and the weight add to the stability factor. Overall the built and design are decent and the grip provides comfortable handling and functioning.

**Display:**  
The back of the device is subjected to a 4.8 inch Super Clear LCD capacitive touch screen with HD resolution of 1280×720 pixels. It provides a total pixel resolution of 17 mega pixels and effective pixel resolution of 16.3 mega pixels. The screen does get dirty by fingerprints and without a viewfinder it is reasonably usable outdoors. With the touch screen being the hero, the gadget does not carry much mechanical or physical buttons.

![Samsung Galaxy Camera ](http://www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/samsung-galaxy-camera-new.jpg)

**Image Quality and Features:**  
With the 21x optical zoom lens you can capture shots at a wide angle and from a great distance. At 23 mm focal length, it’s an additional bonus for taking group shots in less area. The Galaxy Camera is capable of taking an in-focus click after waking up from standby mode in just 2.5 seconds. This is not it. It can even capture 20 shots at 3.7 frames per second.

It has 3 modes for capturing your best moments: Auto, Smart and Expert. The smart mode consists of a Panorama mode that can shoot 20 continuous photos in five seconds for fast moving objects and then stitch them together. Another is the Best Face mode that takes five images quickly and then let you select the best facial expressions of the people pictured, before merging them all into one photo. This is really helpful tool while capturing group photographs as every individual differ in their expressions. the other modes include, Landscape, Macro, Action freeze, Rich tone, Waterfall, Silhouette, Sunset, Night, Fireworks and Light trace. The Expert mode endows the users with P (Auto+), A (Aperture Priority), S (Speed Priority), Camcorder, M (Manual) options, which are controlled via a virtual lens barrel on the screen.

The other features include optical image stabilization, red eye reduction, auto flash and more. The optical image stabilization helps in reducing the blur caused by shaky hands. This makes hand-held shooting possible at high zooms possible and easy.

For recording mono audio it has a microphone present in the camera. The camera is also equipped with a Xenon flash on the left top that is covered into the body and pops up using a mechanical button located on the left of the camera.

**Video Recording:**  
The Samsung Galaxy Camera GC100 has all the video options that you would find on a high-end android device. It provides the users with full HD 1080p and 720p video recording at 30 frames per second with autofocus. It is also capable of recording slow motion video that records at an impressive 120fps and gives a 4x slow motion effect when played back. There’s also the option to apply a variety of filters to video recordings. The colors produced are good but the noise is relatively high. The zoom works smoothly and silently and does not produce any audio disturbance during recording.  
[![samsung-galaxy-camera-new2](http://www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/samsung-galaxy-camera-new2.jpg)](http://www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/samsung-galaxy-camera-new2.jpg)

**Battery life:**  
The camera comes with a 1650 mAh battery, and can take up to 350 shots (without flash), or even record up to an hour and a half of video. It provides a battery backup of 6 hours of video playback, 34 hours of music playback, or 6 hours of internet usage and a standby time of 280 hours.  
Android Experience and Performance:  
The Galaxy Camera runs Android 4.1.1 (Jelly Bean) and is power driven by a quad-core CPU running at 1.4 GHz, which is quite efficient for all your camera needs. Out of 8 GB of storage present, only 4 GB is user-accessible and can be used to store photos and movies. The microSD card slot can provide additional storage up to 64 GB.

[![Samsung Galaxy Camera ](http://www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/samsung-galaxy-camera-new3.jpg)](http://www.itvizkids.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/samsung-galaxy-camera-new3.jpg)

**Apps and Sharing:**  
Photo Wizard is a pre-loaded creative app that provides on-the-go image editing and Video Editor. You can also delete and rearrange video scenes, add music and insert text. Another application is the Paper Artist that gives you a selection of artistic effects to exhibit your creativity. It is also loaded with the favorite and most important application, Instagram.  
The sharing of images can be done over a Wi-Fi connection or even a 3G/4G network if your device is set with a micro-SIM and a suitable data plan. Galaxy Camera GC100 comes ready to share via Email, Google+, Dropbox, or direct via Bluetooth. It automatically creates a backup of images to the cloud over 3G or 4G networks.

Verdict:  
The Samsung Galaxy Camera GC100 combines the functions of an Android device and a dedicated camera quite well, letting you share your photos and videos to the Web instantly. The [Samsung Galaxy Camera GC100 price](http://www.naaptol.com/digital-cameras/samsung-galaxy-camera-gc100/p/12166635.html) in the India is Rs 21,999. So for an experience that stays with your forever with great pictures and videos, this camera is definitely a good option.
